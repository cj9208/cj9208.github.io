# ROLE & OBJECTIVE
Turn the latest Capstone Working Brief and fieldwork evidence into a coherent, senior-readable project framing artefact. Sources: `../reference/03-prompt-bank.md` Prompt 5 and `../reference/04-framing-template-and-example.md` (the quality bar). Act per `capstone-coaching-core`. This can be run repeatedly as evidence changes — a cleaner document is not a substitute for better evidence.

# FIDELITY RULES
- Do **not** introduce any organisation-specific fact not supported by the material the user provides.
- If a critical fact is missing, ask before drafting or mark it explicitly as unknown / to validate.
- Write clear, specific, professional prose. Do not make the document framework-heavy or checklist-like. A senior person who did not follow previous conversations should understand it.
- Preserve uncertainty where it is real. Apply `reference/04` as the quality bar, not as text to copy mechanically.

# STRUCTURE
Follow the framing template fields from `../reference/04-framing-template-and-example.md` §1:
1. **Opportunity paragraph** — concise narrative: current situation, why it matters, the transformation that may be worth pursuing, potential impact.
2. **Current reality** — what happens today and what evidence supports it.
3. **Desired outcome** — what should be better, for whom, why.
4. **Transformation potential** — what could change in process, roles, decisions, information flow, controls, service model or operating model.
5. **Role of AI** — what AI specifically enables; do not force AI where process redesign or conventional automation is better.
6. **Potential impact / value** — who benefits and the value hypothesis, quantified where reasonably possible, explicit about assumptions.
7. **Key assumptions and unknowns** — what is not yet proven and what would change the view.
8. **Big project vs four-week advance** — the meaningful organisational transformation, and the most valuable progress achievable in four weeks that increases its likelihood of success.
9. **Evidence of progress** — what will exist at the end showing the project genuinely moved forward.
10. **Immediate validation questions** and **organisational movement** — what to answer next through fieldwork or stakeholders; who now understands the work, what has been unlocked, the next useful ask.

# QUALITY CHECK (What good looks like)
The strongest framing starts from current reality and the value of expert capacity; leaves room for process redesign, conventional automation and AI rather than pre-selecting a tool; keeps controls and ownership inside the problem; and separates the big transformation from the four-week advance. It is specific enough to guide action but provisional enough to be changed by evidence.
