**Project Framing Template and Worked Example**

What good looks like for a transformation opportunity

This template helps turn a vague idea into a clear transformation
opportunity. It separates the larger project from the most valuable
four-week advance, and it keeps AI as one possible enabler rather than
the whole project.

**1. Project framing template**

  **Field**                        **What to include**
  -------------------------------- ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Opportunity paragraph            A short paragraph describing the opportunity, why it matters, what transformation may be possible, how AI may contribute, and what impact could result.
  Current reality                  What happens today? Where is the friction, cost, delay, risk, quality issue or missed opportunity? What evidence exists?
  Desired outcome                  What should be better if the project succeeds?
  Transformation potential         What could change in the process, roles, decisions, information flow, controls, service model or operating model?
  Role of AI                       What does AI specifically enable? Do not force AI where process redesign or conventional automation is better.
  Potential impact                 Who benefits and what kind of value may be created?
  Key assumptions and unknowns     What is not yet proven? What would change your view?
  Big project                      The meaningful organisational transformation you ultimately want to advance.
  Four-week advance                The most valuable progress you can make in four weeks to increase the likelihood that the big project succeeds.
  Evidence of progress             What will exist at the end that shows the project genuinely moved forward?
  Immediate validation questions   What questions should be answered next through fieldwork, evidence or stakeholder conversations?

**2. Worked example: tax information processing**

**Weak framing**

  -----------------------------------------
  Use AI to automate tax form processing.
  -----------------------------------------

**Stronger opportunity paragraph**

A tax team spends thousands of hours extracting and consolidating
information from multiple forms before specialists can do the
higher-value tax analysis and optimisation work they are actually
trained for. The opportunity is to redesign how tax information moves
from source documents to expert judgement, so that routine preparation
requires far less specialist time while review, control and
accountability remain appropriate. AI may help extract, classify and
structure information, but the larger transformation is about
reallocating professional capacity from repetitive preparation towards
judgement-intensive tax work. If successful, the organisation could
recover substantial specialist capacity, reduce turnaround time, improve
consistency and enable tax experts to focus on higher-value
optimisation.

**Structured framing**

  **Field**                  **Example**
  -------------------------- -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Current reality            Specialists spend large amounts of time extracting and consolidating form data before they can analyse tax positions. The team believes the preparation burden is material, but the precise breakdown by task still needs validation.
  Desired outcome            Reduce repetitive preparation work and increase the time specialists spend on tax analysis and optimisation.
  Transformation potential   Redesign the preparation-to-analysis workflow: intake, extraction, exception handling, review, quality checks and hand-off into expert judgement.
  Role of AI                 Potentially extract, classify and structure information from documents, flag exceptions and support review. AI is an enabler, not the whole transformation.
  Potential impact           Recovered expert capacity, faster turnaround, more consistent preparation, and more time for higher-value tax optimisation.
  Key assumptions            The workload is concentrated in repeatable preparation steps; source documents are structured enough to support AI-assisted extraction; controls can be maintained; specialists will trust the redesigned workflow.
  Big project                Redesign the tax information-processing workflow so that scarce tax expertise is focused on judgement and optimisation rather than repetitive preparation.
  Four-week advance          Establish a credible case for redesign by validating where time is spent, identifying which steps require tax expertise, mapping the current workflow, testing a representative slice of the redesigned approach, and determining whether leadership has enough evidence to support the next stage.
  Evidence of progress       Validated baseline, current workflow map, redesigned workflow draft, evidence from a representative test or walkthrough, list of controls/constraints, and a clear recommendation for the next decision.

**3. Why the stronger example is better**

> • It starts from professional capacity and organisational value, not
> from the AI tool.
>
> • It treats AI as one enabler inside a broader transformation.
>
> • It distinguishes the big project from the four-week advance.
>
> • The four-week advance is not merely a technical milestone; it
> creates evidence, learning and decision readiness.
>
> • It keeps assumptions visible rather than overstating certainty.
